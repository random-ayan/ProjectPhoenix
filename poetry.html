The First Meeting

23th May 2023

I met her in the debris Of my fate, I felt her, but I knew- It was late. From the Moon to the Sun, From the dusk till the dawn, I got someone who was yet To be carried on.

The First Kiss

13th December 2023

The stars, shining brighter, The old yellow moon with a broad smile, And the elegant silence of the darkness Softly whispered the presence of night. The Aroma of your hair, And the glimpse of your eyes. The destruction i need, Thus they invite. With my head bowed down, And your chin, uplifted so close, I've lost my whole destiny Within the divine lips of yours.
